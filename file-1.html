HOW TO SURVIVE

🎮 KEYBOARD CONTROLS

  • A / D or ← → : Move Left / Right
  • SPACE / W / ↑ : Jump (Hold higher, tap shorter)
  • R : Quick Restart Level
  • ESC : Pause Game
  • M : Mute / Unmute Audio

📱 TOUCH CONTROLS

On mobile phones and tablets, responsive left/right buttons appear on the bottom-left, and a large JUMP button on the bottom-right.

💡 PRO PLATFORMING TIPS

  • Coyote Time: You can still jump for a split-second after walking off an edge.
  • Jump Buffering: Pressing jump right before landing will execute automatically.
  • Variable Jumps: Release the jump button early to execute low, controlled hops.
  • Head Collision: Solid blocks will bump your head down. Time your trajectory!

⚠️ HAZARD GUIDE

  • Crimson Spikes: Instant death. Don't touch tips or sides.
  • Crumbling Platforms: Stand on them, and they shatter within 0.4s!
  • Bounce Pads: Launches you high into the sky.
  • Checkpoints: Reach the neon flag to respawn there on death.
